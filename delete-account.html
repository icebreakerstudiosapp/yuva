Delete your Yuva account and data

Yuva, provided by Ice Breaker Studios. Last updated: 14 November 2026

You can delete your Yuva account and the data stored with it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Yuva and tap Settings in the bottom bar.
  2. Scroll down to Account and tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm. Yuva deletes your data from our servers and from your phone, then closes.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email icebreakerstudios.app@outlook.com with the subject “Delete my Yuva account”. Write the Google account email you linked to Yuva so we can find your profile.

Profiles that were never linked to a Google account are tied only to an anonymous ID stored on your phone. We cannot match such a profile to you by email. Use Option 1 while the app is still installed.

What is deleted

  • Your profile on our servers: pet, name, budget and the apps in it, streak, cosmetics and settings.
  • Your Yuva sign-in account (anonymous or Google-linked).
  • All Yuva data stored on your phone (when you use Option 1).

What may remain

  • Analytics data collected automatically by Google Analytics for Firebase is not part of your profile. It is kept and removed according to Google’s retention settings and does not contain your pet or budget.
  • Copies made by Android’s device backup, if you enabled it, are controlled by Google and your device settings.

See also our Privacy Policy.
