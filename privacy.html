Yuva Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Yuva (“the app”) is an Android app that helps you keep a daily budget for the time you spend in other apps, with a little animal that waits for you. The app is provided by Ice Breaker Studios (“we”). Contact: icebreakerstudios.app@outlook.com.

In short

  • The time you spend in each app is read on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Your account stores only your pet profile, your budget and your streak (Google Firebase).
  • We show no ads, we do not sell your data, and we share it with no one except Google, which runs our infrastructure.

1. Data we process

Stays on your device only

  • Time spent in each app, read through Android’s “Usage access” permission, and daily totals for the last 30 days (used for your statistics, reminders and home-screen widget).
  • Local caches of your budget and settings used by the reminder service and the widget.

Stored in your account on our servers (Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, EU region)

  • An anonymous user ID that is created automatically the first time you open the app.
  • Your pet’s species and name, your daily total limit, and the apps in your budget (package name, display name, colour and per-app limit).
  • Your streak, best streak, warning and recovery progress, date and time zone of the last daily check, break (sleep) mode status, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, adopted-pet history and pending budget changes.

Only if you choose “Continue with Google”

  • Your Google account identifier and email address, received through Firebase Authentication, so your profile can be restored after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Analytics

  • The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase, which automatically collects an app-instance identifier, device and app information (model, OS and app version), approximate country, and session data. We do not log custom events about which apps you use or for how long.

2. Permissions and why we ask

  • Usage access: to measure how long you use the apps in your budget. Required for the daily budget to work.
  • Display over other apps: to show a short reminder screen when you open a budgeted app past its limit. The app does not read the content of other apps or of your screen.
  • Notifications: to warn you when you approach or pass a limit and to send a daily summary.
  • Foreground service and start at boot: so reminders keep working in the background and after your phone restarts.
  • Installed apps list: to let you choose which apps to budget. Only the apps you add to your budget are saved to your account.

Usage access, display over other apps and notifications are optional. You can turn them off in Android settings at any time; the related features will stop working.

3. Android backup

Android’s automatic backup may copy the app’s local data (for example the local daily totals) to your Google account, depending on your device’s backup settings. This is handled by Google and your device, not by us.

4. Sharing

We use Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Analytics for Firebase) to run the app. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not share your data with any other third party.

5. Retention and deletion

Your profile is kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the app does not delete data stored in your account. You can delete your account and data yourself in the app (Settings → Account → Delete my account and data), or follow the steps on our account deletion page, which also explains how to request deletion by email at icebreakerstudios.app@outlook.com. Profiles that were never linked to a Google account cannot be matched to you by us by email.

6. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections. Our database rules allow each profile to be read and written only by its own signed-in user.

7. Children

Yuva is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: icebreakerstudios.app@outlook.com.
